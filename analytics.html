        How this is counted

        The site adds one to a handful of anonymous tallies when someone opens it: the total, the day, the kind of device, where they came from and their part of the world. It also counts when the résumé is opened, a LinkedIn link is clicked or the fireworks are launched (– so far).

        No names, addresses, cookies or ids are stored, so this page cannot tell you who a visitor was. The tallies live on two free public counter services. They need no account, but they come with no guarantee, and someone who reads the page's code could add to them.
